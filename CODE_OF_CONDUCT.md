# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being respectful of different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing on what is best for the people using the project;
- showing empathy and kindness.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information, such as a phone number, address or a customer's data, without explicit permission;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@boardroom-ai.ae**. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor whose behaviour is inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
